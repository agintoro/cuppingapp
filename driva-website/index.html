Our Story

Born from the
Soil of Passion

Driva Coffee began as a commitment — not to volume, but to truth. In the highlands of Indonesia and the valleys of East Africa, we partner directly with farmers who share our belief: that great coffee is never an accident.

Every lot we release tells the story of a specific place, a specific harvest, and the hands that tended it. We cup every batch rigorously — tracking aroma, flavour, aftertaste, acidity, body, balance, and uniformity — before a single gram leaves our facility.

"We don't sell coffee. We translate terroir — the whisper of soil, altitude, and season — into every cup."

Quality Control

Scored to Excellence

The SCA cupping protocol is our quality language. Ten attributes, each scored 6–10, combine to form a final score that determines whether a lot earns its place in our lineup. We share full transparency reports with every purchase.

Our minimum release standard is 87 points — the lower threshold of specialty coffee excellence. Our limited lots often exceed 91. We publish the full cupping sheet so you can taste with context.

Why Driva

A Different Kind of Roaster

🌿

Direct Trade

We bypass intermediaries entirely. Our prices ensure farmers receive 30–50% above commodity market rates, building long-term relationships and improving lives across our supply chain.

🔬

Scientific Precision

Every roast is logged: inlet temperature, charge, rate of rise, first crack time, development ratio. We replicate excellence consistently and refine with each batch.

📄

Full Traceability

Know your farmer, their altitude, their harvest date, their processing method. We publish the complete provenance chain — from seed to your door.

🌍

Seasonal Releases

We don't maintain year-round lots. We chase the world's harvests, releasing coffees at peak freshness in limited quantities that respect the rhythm of each origin.

♻️

Carbon Conscious

Certified carbon-neutral roasting since 2023. Recyclable packaging, offset freight emissions, and a commitment to zero landfill by 2026.

🎓

Education First

Every order ships with a brew guide tuned to that specific lot — grind size, ratio, temperature, and tasting notes to guide your exploration.
